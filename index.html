How Vers Speed Works

Vers Speed performs a realistic, high-precision test of your Internet connection using global edge CDN servers:

  • Download Speed: Measures how fast data travels from servers to your device with parallel data streams.
  • Latency (Unloaded): The round-trip time of a request when there is no other traffic on your network.
  • Latency (Loaded): Measures responsiveness and bufferbloat under heavy data transfer.
  • Upload Speed: Measures how fast your device uploads data to the cloud.

Tip: Close other bandwidth-heavy apps or downloads during the test for the most accurate result.
